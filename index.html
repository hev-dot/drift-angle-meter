Measure your drift angle

Uses your phone's motion sensors and GPS. Nothing to install, and it calibrates itself for your phone.

  1. Mount the phone firmly, in any orientation. It must not move while you drive.
  2. Tap Start and allow motion and location access.
  3. Keep the car still for about 3 seconds.
  4. Drive normally for about a minute: a few turns, some accelerating and braking.
  5. Once the gauge is live, go drift.

Keep the screen on and the browser in front while measuring. Only drift on closed courses or other places where it's legal and safe. Don't look at the phone while driving.
